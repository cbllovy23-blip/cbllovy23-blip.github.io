📝 본문 (Description)

I boarded a cruise departing from Hinode Pier and spent the evening taking in the nighttime scenery of Tokyo Bay from the deck.

After a light meal, I spent most of the cruise out on deck with the camera. The Rainbow Bridge, Tokyo Tower, and the Odaiba skyline all look completely different when viewed from the water.

A perspective you simply can't get from land — one of the most memorable moments of this Tokyo trip.

히노데 부두 (Hinode Pier): https://maps.app.goo.gl/yVdcfXJba7MYdHvk8

Hinode Pier in Minato is one of Tokyo's main water transport hubs, offering water buses to Odaiba and Asakusa as well as a range of night cruise options — from full dinner cruises to lighter packages with drinks only.

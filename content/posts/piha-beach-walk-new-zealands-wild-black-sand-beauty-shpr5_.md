Piha Beach is Auckland's most famous west coast beach, characterized by fine black sand formed from volcanic ash.

The massive 'Lion Rock' rising in the middle of the beach is an iconic landmark, and the powerful waves make it a world-renowned surfing destination.

Surrounded by sheer cliffs and lush forests, it is considered one of the best places to witness the wild beauty of New Zealand.

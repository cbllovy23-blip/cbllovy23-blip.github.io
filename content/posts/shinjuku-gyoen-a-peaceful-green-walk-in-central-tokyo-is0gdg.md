In early June 2026, I took a leisurely walk through the entirety of Shinjuku Gyoen — a vast green oasis right in the heart of Tokyo.

From wide open lawns to a traditional Japanese garden, and French and English formal gardens — the variety packed into this single park is quietly impressive for a city setting.

What struck me most was seeing tourists and locals alike — each enjoying the space in their own unhurried way.

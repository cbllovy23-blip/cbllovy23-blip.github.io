I walked through Senso-ji Temple and the surrounding streets of Asakusa, Tokyo — from daytime all the way through to night.

By day, Senso-ji buzzes with visitors passing through Kaminarimon and along the Nakamise shopping street — the temple grounds filled with touches of traditional Japanese culture at every turn.

After dark, Senso-ji takes on a completely different character. With fewer crowds and soft lighting across the grounds, the nighttime atmosphere carries a quieter, more contemplative charm.

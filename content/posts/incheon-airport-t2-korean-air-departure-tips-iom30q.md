**Getting to Incheon Airport T2 (AREX Direct Express):**
Seoul Station -> Incheon Airport T2 takes about 51 minutes on the AREX direct express train (no stops). Trains run roughly every 30-40 minutes, first train around 06:00, last train around 22:50 (schedules do shift, so check current times before you go). Walk-up fare is about 18,000-18,300 won one way; booking ahead through an online travel platform is often noticeably cheaper (commonly 11,000-13,000 won). The slower all-stop (일반열차) train is cheaper (about 4,500-4,850 won) but takes close to an hour and makes several stops -- fine if you're not in a rush.

If you're flying Korean Air or Asiana, you can check in and drop your bags right at Seoul Station's City Airport Terminal before boarding the train -- recommended arrival is 3 hours before your flight. One thing to watch for: once you've checked in, the AREX gate reads your specific train ticket, and it stops letting that ticket through a couple of minutes before departure, since there's a minimum time needed to get from the check-in level down to the platform. Give yourself a real buffer rather than aiming to scan in right at the cutoff -- if you miss it, you'll be rebooked onto the next train for free, but you'll lose 30-40 minutes waiting around.

**Korean Air check-in / arrival at T2:**
Korean Air's main departure area is on the 3rd floor. Economy bag drop is at Counters B and C (Counter C handles oversized items like golf clubs, bikes, and pets); Prestige/First and SkyPriority check-in is at Counters A1-A2. Self-service bag drop is available at Counter B but doesn't accept large items or pets. If you already checked in at Seoul Station, look for the simplified/express exit set aside for pre-checked passengers -- it's easy to walk past, so if you can't spot it quickly, the regular line usually moves about as fast anyway. Picking up duty-free purchases afterward: the kiosk ticket machine will try to make you log into an app for a digital queue number, but scanning your passport instead gets you a number without the extra step.

**Heads up if you're flying Asiana:** since Korean Air's merger with Asiana Airlines, Asiana has also moved into T2 -- it relocated from T1 on January 14, 2026. Asiana's counters are on the east side at H and J (separate from Korean Air's A-D counters above), so double-check which terminal your ticket actually says before you leave for the airport; a shuttle runs between T1 and T2 if you end up at the wrong one, but it costs you time you don't want to lose.

**Free performances in the terminal:** Incheon Airport runs rotating cultural performances (traditional gugak music, talchum mask dance, that kind of thing) in the T2 departure hall, generally in the gate-240s/250s area, a couple of times a day. It's free and open to anyone passing through, but the exact schedule and location shift with the season, so check the info desk or the airport's event listings on the day -- don't plan your connection around a specific showtime you saw online.

**Baggage allowance (Korean Air economy):** Carry-on is one bag plus one personal item, combined weight 10 kg or under, with the three sides summing to 115 cm or less. Checked baggage is one piece up to 23 kg on most international routes (two pieces up to 23 kg each on US and Brazil routes), three sides summing to 158 cm or less. Allowances vary by fare type, so double-check what your specific ticket includes before you pack.

**Something to do if you have time after check-in:** T2 isn't just gates and duty-free -- there's an indoor garden, installation art scattered around the terminal, exhibition halls, an observation deck, and a media art display, plus Hansik Midam-gil, a food street curating dishes from around Korea. Worth a wander if you've got the usual extra hour before boarding.

In early June 2026, this video follows the journey from a stunning aerial view of Mt. Fuji on the flight from Gimpo to Haneda, through the terminal on arrival, and all the way to the duty-free zone before heading back to Seoul.

The aerial view of Mt. Fuji through the cabin window is the first highlight of this video — a lucky clear-day glimpse that adds to the excitement of the journey.

Haneda Airport is close to central Tokyo and, despite its size, is well-organized and easy to navigate — even for first-time visitors.

On the afternoon of departure, after checking in, I took a final stroll through the duty-free area in the international departure zone — a fitting farewell to Haneda.

Haneda Airport (Tokyo International Airport) is located in Ota, Tokyo. The Gimpo–Haneda route connects two city-center airports, making it one of the most convenient routes between Seoul and Tokyo.

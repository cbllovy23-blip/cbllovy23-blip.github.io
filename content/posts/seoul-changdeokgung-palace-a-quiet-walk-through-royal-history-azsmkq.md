Changdeokgung Palace Information:

Changdeokgung Palace, meaning 'Prospering Virtue Palace,' was built in 1405 and served as a secondary palace for Joseon kings.

It's renowned for its harmonious integration with the surrounding natural landscape, particularly its secret garden, Biwon.

Unlike other palaces, its layout follows the natural contours of the land, making it unique and picturesque.

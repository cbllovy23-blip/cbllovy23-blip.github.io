In February 2026, take a full-loop walk through the history of Suwon at Suwon Hwaseong Fortress, accompanied by the calm winter air.

Suwon Hwaseong Fortress, built during the reign of King Jeongjo of the Joseon Dynasty, is a UNESCO World Heritage site recognized for its military defense capabilities and architectural beauty.

Starting from Hwasomun Gate, I captured the refreshing views while walking along the grand stretch of the fortress walls.

True to the saying "Even Geumgangsan is best seen after eating," I snapped photos of Suwon's famous King Galbi Chicken before soaking in the vibes of Haengnidan-gil, filled with stylish cafes and charming shops.

Sharing a perfect Suwon walking route that begins with historical aesthetics, captures trendy alleyway vibes, and circles back to Hwasomun.

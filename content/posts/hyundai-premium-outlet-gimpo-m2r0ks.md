**Practical info:**
Hours run roughly 10:30-20:00 or 10:30-21:00 depending on the day and season (open year-round, no regular closed days) -- check the current hours before you go since they shift. Address: 100 Arayuk-ro 152beon-gil, Gochon-eup, Gimpo-si, Gyeonggi-do (10135). On-site parking is available. The West building's food court includes Texas Roadhouse, Outback, and Dore Dore, among others. For current shuttle bus or public-transit routes, the outlet's official site (ehyundai.com) or customer service (1588-3650) can confirm, since schedules aren't listed on third-party pages.

**Saturday-night fireworks show:** The outlet runs a recurring riverside fireworks show (리버사이드 불꽃축제) on weekend evenings, at the waterfront on the 1st floor of the West building -- historically around 7:30 PM and about 10 minutes long. Part of the appeal is watching it with the Han River as a backdrop. Dates and exact times shift by season and get cancelled in rain, so confirm the current schedule with the outlet before planning your visit around it.

**A note from actually shopping there:** if you time it right, you can get really good deals -- non-refundable (final sale) items sometimes show up cheap during end-of-season clearance.

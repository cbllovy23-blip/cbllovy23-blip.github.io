Experience a quiet moment with art at the Gothenburg Museum of Art, in the heart of Gothenburg, Sweden.

Various artworks from different eras and genres, captured silently, guide you into the world of art.

The Gothenburg Museum of Art is one of the most important art museums in Northern Europe, housing an extensive art collection spanning from the 15th century to the present.

It is particularly renowned for Nordic art, especially Swedish artworks from the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

Cheong Wa Dae (Blue House) Information:

Cheong Wa Dae, or the Blue House, served as the executive office and official residence of the South Korean head of state from 1948 to 2022.

Known for its distinctive blue tiled roofs, it is set against the backdrop of Bugaksan Mountain, offering a blend of traditional Korean architecture and natural beauty.

Since its opening to the public, it has become a significant historical and cultural landmark, attracting visitors eager to explore its grounds and learn about its past.

I captured the scenery of the majestic three-tiered waterfall while walking along the trail through the lush native forest.

Kitekite Falls is a representative waterfall in Auckland, located in the Waitākere Ranges Regional Park, standing approximately 40 meters tall with three tiers.

Accessible via the Kitekite Track starting near Piha Beach, it is a beloved hiking course where visitors can walk while observing New Zealand's unique and lush native flora.

Take a walk along the cherry blossom path near Pangyo Hwarang Park, blooming brilliantly under the April sun.

I captured the endless pink cherry blossom tunnels along Geumtocheon, a small stream flowing from Pangyo Station toward Hwarang Park.

You can enjoy Pangyo's unique and sophisticated spring scenery, where clear water flows amidst the modern IT Valley buildings framed by lush cherry blossoms.

Sharing the record of the dazzling cherry blossom trails encountered in April, the most beautiful season in Pangyo.

https://maps.app.goo.gl/1pkjt5EJ67U3nvE89

The Geumtocheon Cherry Blossom Path is a trail along the stream that cuts through the heart of Pangyo Techno Valley. Every April, it becomes a hidden gem offering perfect relaxation for office workers and residents.

It is great for flower viewing while listening to the sound of water, as the cherry trees hang low on both sides of the stream.

I recommend the view from the small bridges over the stream to capture both the "forest" of buildings and the cherry blossom tunnel in one shot.

The spacious lawn and lake at Hwarang Park, located at the end of the trail, are perfect for a spring picnic.

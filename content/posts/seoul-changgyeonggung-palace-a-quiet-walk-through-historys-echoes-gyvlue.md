Changgyeonggung Palace Information:

Changgyeonggung Palace, built in 1483, served primarily as a residential palace for queens and concubines during the Joseon Dynasty.

Unlike other grand palaces, it possesses a more intimate and delicate charm, reflecting its role.

It's known for its poignant history and beautiful, serene atmosphere, offering a unique glimpse into royal life.

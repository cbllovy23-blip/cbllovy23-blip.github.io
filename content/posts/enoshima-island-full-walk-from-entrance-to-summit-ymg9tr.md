📝 본문 (Description)

After walking from Kamakura, I arrived at Enoshima Island and explored it from the entrance all the way up to the summit.

From crossing Benten Bridge at the entrance, through the shopping street, up the steps past the shrine area, and finally to the open views at the summit — the full journey from one end to the other.

On a clear day, the panoramic view of Sagami Bay from the summit — with the distant silhouette of Mt. Fuji — is one of the best reasons to make the climb.

Enoshima is a small island about 4km in circumference located in Fujisawa, Kanagawa Prefecture, connected to the mainland by a bridge. With the Enoshima Shrine, sea caves (Iwaya), and observation towers all within walking distance, it makes for a satisfying half-day trip.

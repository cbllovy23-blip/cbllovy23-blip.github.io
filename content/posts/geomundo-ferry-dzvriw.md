**Getting to Geomundo by ferry -- two routes, pick based on your situation:**

*Via Nokdong New Port (녹동신항), Goheung:* Fewer sailings but cheaper -- and there's no flexibility, since it's only one round trip a day. The boat leaves Nokdong at 07:00, arriving Geomundo 08:00 (about 30,000 won one way), and heads back from Geomundo at 13:20, arriving Nokdong 14:20 (about 28,500 won one way). That gives you roughly 5 hours 20 minutes on the island if you're doing it as a day trip -- and since the outbound leg is the only one that day, missing that 07:00 departure means you're not getting to Geomundo via this route at all. Nokdong itself is a small rural port -- you'll basically need a car or taxi to get there. Right by the terminal there's free public parking and restrooms, plus one nearby restaurant (a grilled-eel place), but not much else around.

*Via Yeosu, direct:* The Hamel (하멜) ferry runs Yeosu <-> Geomundo directly, with more sailings and more flexibility. Yeosu -> Geomundo departs 07:30 (arriving 09:13) or 13:10 (arriving 14:53); Geomundo -> Yeosu departs 10:20 (arriving 12:03) or 15:40 (arriving 17:23) -- each leg about 1 hour 43 minutes, from roughly 46,500 won one way, noticeably more than the Nokdong route. That means a day trip can be as short as about 1 hour on the island (07:30 in, 10:20 back) or as long as about 6.5 hours (07:30 in, 15:40 back). The tradeoff for the higher fare is convenience: Yeosu has its own train station and airport, so it's a much easier base to reach and stay in the night before.

Either way: schedules shift with the season, so check the current timetable before you lock in plans. Tickets can be booked through 가보고 싶은 섬 (island.theksa.co.kr, run by the Korea Shipping Association) -- worth booking ahead on weekends or in peak season. You'll need a government ID (resident registration card or driver's license) to board; minors need a family register extract (등본). Boarding closes 10 minutes before departure on both routes, so plan to be at the terminal at least 30 minutes early.

Since you likely won't make it back the same day on at least one of these routes, plan on staying over -- there are minbak (small family-run guesthouses) on the island. There's no single site that aggregates Geomundo listings, so just search around online and book ahead.

**One important caution with any remote island like this:** ferries can be cancelled on short notice if the weather turns -- fog, high wind, or rough swell are enough, and the call is made by the maritime authorities, not the ferry operator, so it's not something you can talk your way around. Because Geomundo only gets one or two sailings a day, a cancellation doesn't just mean a delay -- it can mean being stuck on the island for several more days until conditions clear and a boat actually runs. Build slack into your schedule (don't book a flight or anything time-sensitive for the day right after you plan to leave), and check the day's departure status before you leave your accommodation.

**A few notes from actually being there:**
If you get seasick easily, go for the car ferry (차도선) instead of the fast boat -- it's a lot steadier.

Check the weather before you go. It matters more out here than you'd think.

There's also a bit of a hiking trail if you're up for it.

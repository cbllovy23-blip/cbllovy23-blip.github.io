Recorded the nostalgic daytime scenery of Moraenae Market, a place filled with traces of time near Gajwa Station.

Found at the end of the Gajwa section of the Gyeongui Line Forest Park, this old market is small in size but perfectly preserves the atmosphere of old Seoul amidst the modern city.

I captured the sincere daily lives of the market people flowing through the narrow alleys and under the weathered signs.

Sharing the simple and humble scenery of Moraenae Market, which holds a familiar and comfortable charm rather than flashiness.

https://maps.app.goo.gl/1T23dKw7d12EVUheA

Moraenae Market is a traditional market located in Namgajwa-dong, Seodaemun-gu, with its name originating from the nearby Moraenae Stream (the old name for Hongjecheon).

I spent a full day walking through Shibuya, Tokyo — from afternoon to night. Naturally drifting back and forth around the famous Scramble Crossing, I captured the many different faces of this iconic neighborhood.

The Scramble buzzing with daytime energy, quiet side streets in between, then the same intersection transformed under neon lights at night — the way Shibuya shifts with the hours is something else.

No particular plan — just walking, and somehow covering all of Shibuya by the end of the day.

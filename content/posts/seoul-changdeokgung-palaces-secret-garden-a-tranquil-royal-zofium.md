The Secret Garden, or Biwon, is a vast, beautifully preserved traditional Korean garden within Changdeokgung Palace.

It was designed to blend seamlessly with the natural landscape, featuring ponds, pavilions, and diverse flora.

Historically, it served as a private resting place for the royal family, offering an exclusive glimpse into their serene lives.

📝 본문 (Description)

I visited the iconic railway crossing near Kamakurakōkōmae Station — famous as the opening scene of Slam Dunk — then walked the rest of the way toward Fujisawa in the direction of Enoshima.

The scenery along the Enoden (Enoshima Electric Railway) tracks running beside the sea feels like stepping directly into the pages of the manga.

The coastal and townscape views captured while walking from the crossing to Fujisawa add another layer of charm to the journey.

The railway crossing near Kamakurakōkōmae Station became a pilgrimage site for fans worldwide after being featured in the opening sequence of the basketball manga Slam Dunk — the view of the sea beyond the crossing is the iconic shot.
